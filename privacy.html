Privacy Policy

How Cool It Air Conditioning Repair collects, uses and protects your information.

Effective date: [DEMO: effective date]

Cool It Air Conditioning Repair ("we," "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote, call, text or email us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your ZIP code and service address
  • Details about your air conditioning system and the service you need
  • Anything else you choose to include in your message

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics tools we use may record basic technical information such as your browser type, device, pages visited and approximate location based on your IP address. This helps us understand how the site is used and keep it working well.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and contact you about your service
  • To schedule appointments and perform the work you request
  • To send estimates, invoices and service follow-ups
  • To improve our website and customer service
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We only share your information when needed to provide our services or run our business, for example with:

  • Service providers that help us operate, such as our website host, form delivery service, email provider and scheduling or invoicing software
  • Equipment manufacturers or distributors, when needed to register a warranty on equipment we install for you
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights and safety

These providers may only use your information to perform services for us.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies or similar tools to measure traffic and improve the site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work without them.

Phone calls and text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your quote or service appointment. We will not send you marketing text messages without your consent. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text message to opt out.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. No website or internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep information

We keep your information as long as needed to provide service, maintain customer and warranty records, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Links to other websites

Our site may link to other websites, such as Google reviews or social media. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
